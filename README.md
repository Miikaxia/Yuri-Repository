# The Yuri Repository (Feat. Yaoi)
Hii this is a repository for notes, screenshots, photos, ect. about my EIF task about misconceptions and taboos around LGBTQIA+ people and media.

## What does this mean?
This repository is like a digital folder for my research and notes for this task.

Definitions:
Misconceptions: Potential misunderstandings, confusions, and assumptions that could be made.
Taboos: The cultral, social, or otherwise alienation of something, residing in a somewhat contreversial grey area.
LGBTQIA+: Anything referring to queer or non typically aligned relating to personal identity, sexuality, gender, ect.
Media: Online, physical, or otherwise mediums of media, including but not limited to literature, arts, music, other forms of pop culture, ect.

## Intentions.
I intend to educate a class on LGBTQIA+ media and individuals to clarify any common misconceptions and taboos surrounding them. Through explanation, answering questions and proof of research. Aided with examples, interview transcripts, and ideally, enquiry based learning.
