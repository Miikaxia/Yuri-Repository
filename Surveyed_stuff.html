Why do some people feel the need to resort to niche terms to describe their identity, even if there are more common terms/groups that are nearly identical?

Sometimes its about level of detail, while there are very close and more common terms, some people may feel like it doesn't describe them well enough. It's like when you're writing something, and you have to use more esoteric words because you can't find the right way to describe something using more common words. Other times it can be for a sense of individuality. Being group with so many other people can feel restrictive at times, and so being part of a smaller sub-community within the larger community can be a breath of fresh air, as you get less clumped up with others, the specifity of it makes it can reduce misunderstandings or other similar situations. And sometimes, people just like it better and prefer it, maybe it just fits, or it just sounds nice. Occasionally, it's really not that deep.
